Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026

GutClue was designed from the ground up around a strict Local-First, Zero-Surveillance privacy architecture. We believe your sensitive digestive health logs are entirely private to you.

Core Promise: GutClue does not operate user accounts, cloud databases, analytics beacons, or third-party advertising SDKs. Your health data never leaves your device.

1. 100% On-Device Data Storage

All meals, symptoms, medication notes, check-ins, and experiment logs are stored exclusively in a sandboxed SQLite database located on your physical device. No health data is ever transmitted, sold, or shared with external servers, cloud databases, advertisers, or analytics brokers.

2. Zero Trackers & Telemetry

GutClue contains zero third-party behavioral analytics (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or Mixpanel) and zero advertising trackers (such as Google AdMob or Meta SDKs). The application operates with complete network isolation.

3. Backups & Device Migration

  • Device-to-Device Migration: When transferring to a new phone via standard operating system direct cable or Wi-Fi migration, your data transfers directly between your phones.
  • Cloud Backups: The app's database is configured to exclude automatic cloud backups (Google Cloud Backup and Apple iCloud), preserving your data strictly on your local hardware.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you retain full rights over your data:

  • Data Portability (Art. 20): You can export your complete personal logs at any time in structured CSV or JSON format directly from the Settings screen.
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17): Tapping "Delete all my data" in Settings instantly and permanently purges all database tables, reminders, and cached preferences from your device.

5. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the official application store listing page.
